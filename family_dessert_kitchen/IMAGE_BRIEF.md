# Image Brief — The Family Dessert Kitchen
Cristy Sim · optional photography direction

The book **ships complete with original vector illustrations** (all inline SVG, no external or copyrighted
assets). This brief is for an optional future photography pass, should real food images be commissioned.
Do **not** insert any unlicensed image; use only originally shot, licensed, public-domain, or properly
generated imagery, and never copy photographs from the source PDFs or the internet.

## Art direction (aligned to Source A — The Recomp Kitchen)
- **Look:** minimal, bright, editorial. White or very light surfaces, soft natural daylight, gentle shadows.
- **Palette:** charcoal props, one clean blue accent; secondary mango-yellow, strawberry-pink, soft-orange for playfulness.
- **Composition:** generous negative space, top-down or 3/4 angle, single hero portion in a small plain bowl/cup/mold.
- **Mood:** calm, unstyled-but-tidy, real family kitchen; no perfection, no diet cues, no packaging or branding.
- **People:** optional child hands only (measuring, mixing, decorating) — no faces, no identifiable individuals; obtain releases if any person appears.
- **Never:** watermarks, brand packaging, logos, stock-photo clichés, "guilt-free/skinny" styling.

## Shot list (one hero per recipe where budget allows; prioritize chapter openers)
- **Frozen & Fruity:** mango yogurt pops on a light tray; blueberry frozen-yogurt bark broken into pieces; watermelon granita flakes in a small glass.
- **Yogurt/Pudding/Creamy:** layered strawberry cheesecake cup; cocoa chia pudding with a spoon standing up; golden mango-coconut pudding.
- **Muffins/Bites/Bakes:** mini muffins in a pan; no-bake oat bites rolled on parchment.
- **Warm Fruit:** cinnamon-apple crumble in a small dish, steam optional; baked cinnamon peach halves.
- **Cookies/Bars/Squares:** soft cookies cooling on a rack; fudgy cocoa squares cut on a board.
- **Build-Your-Own:** a small station of labeled bowls (yogurt, fruit, crunch) with child hands assembling.

## Technical
- Format: high-resolution RGB, 300 dpi at print size; A4 page wells ~150–180 mm wide.
- Provide both color and a grayscale-safe version check.
- Deliver with descriptive **alt text** per image for the accessibility pass.

## If generating images
- Use a licensed generator with commercial rights; keep prompts consistent with the palette and "minimal editorial daylight" look.
- Review every generated image for artifacts, implausible food, or accidental text/logos before use.
