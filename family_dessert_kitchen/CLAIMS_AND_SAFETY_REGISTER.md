## Claims deliberately NOT made (guardrails honored)
- No calories, macros, protein/fiber/sugar counts, verified yields, or shelf-life durations.
- No claim that any food boosts immunity, burns fat, detoxifies, prevents disease, or improves behavior.
- No labeling of foods as clean, dirty, good, bad, guilt-free, or sinful.
- No hiding vegetables as a trick; no dessert-as-payment for eating other foods.
- No diagnosis, treatment, calorie prescription, or weight-loss guidance for children.
- No fabricated study, statistic, recommendation, or URL. Only the four deep links above (author-provided) and organization-level references are used.
