SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL CHANGES FROM THE ORIGINAL AGREEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------

TITLE & BRANDING
1.  Retitled the document from "ONLINE COACHING AGREEMENT" to
    "PERSONAL TRAINING & BODY RECOMPOSITION AGREEMENT". No redundant
    "Cristy Sim Fitness" text was added, because the logo already carries
    the brand name.
2.  Placed the CS logo centered at the top of the first page.
3.  Applied a premium, minimalist look using the brand café, beige, and
    gold tones as accents only, on a white (high-legibility) background.
4.  Added a discreet footer on every page:
    "CONFIDENTIAL | cristysim.coaching@gmail.com | Page X".
5.  Corrected numbering, spacing, page flow, headings, and signature layout.

SERVICE MODEL (from online-only to in-person)
6.  Redefined Services as PRIMARILY IN-PERSON training:
      - Two (2) sessions per week.
      - Eight (8) sessions per month.
      - Location mutually agreed between Coach and Client.
      - Supported, when necessary, by WhatsApp, email, video, or a
        coaching platform.
7.  Removed references to the service being exclusively online/remote and
    to weekly calls being the main service. Removed original third-party
    platform names (e.g., Lenus, Stripe, Google Meet, Zoom).
8.  Added an explicit statement that no immediate or continuous
    availability is promised.

SESSIONS
9.  Added a "Sessions Are Not Cumulative" section: no rollover, no cash
    value, non-transferable, weekly structure, and no banking of sessions.
10. Session cancellation/rescheduling now requires 12 hours' notice;
    late cancellations and no-shows count as used; >10 minutes late may be
    shortened or counted as used.
11. Added a Vacation and Travel section: service may continue online when
    reasonably possible; vacations do not accumulate sessions, do not
    suspend billing automatically, and do not create credits.

TERM, RENEWAL & BILLING
12. Changed the initial commitment from SIX months to THREE consecutive
    months.
13. Replaced month-to-month renewal with AUTOMATIC RENEWAL every three
    months, with express authorization of recurring quarterly billing.
14. Service cancellation and freeze requests now require written notice to
    cristysim.coaching@gmail.com at least seven (7) business days before
    the next charge (WhatsApp/DMs/verbal do not count as formal notice).
15. Freezes: written approval required, not automatic for vacation,
    medical documentation may be requested, may extend the commitment, and
    are capped at a maximum of 90 days unless otherwise approved.
16. Kept a reasonable early-termination provision (50% of remaining unpaid
    initial-term fees, subject to law) as compensation for reserved
    capacity — not a penalty.
17. Failed/declined payments: added immediate suspension of services,
    sessions, and programming access; retained grace period and lawful
    late-fee cap.
18. Left editable placeholders for start date, three-month fee / monthly
    fee, billing option, and payment method. No fee was invented.

SCOPE, RESULTS & SAFETY
19. Expanded Nutrition scope: educational/general only; no medication or
    supplement prescriptions; disclosure duties; refer to licensed
    professionals when appropriate.
20. Expanded the No-Guarantee clause with the factors affecting results.
21. Adapted Safety for in-person training and added an express clause
    incorporating the separate Informed Consent & Release of Liability by
    reference, with the waiver controlling in case of a direct conflict.
    The full waiver text is NOT reproduced inside this Agreement.

IP, PRIVACY & COMMUNICATION
22. Expanded Intellectual Property protections, including express
    prohibitions on AI reproduction and derivative products.
23. Privacy: no automatic media release and no marketing consent by
    signing; public use of photos/testimonials requires separate written
    consent.
24. Communication: WhatsApp as primary operational channel, email for
    formal notices, 24–36h ordinary response (up to 48h during announced
    travel), emergencies to local services.

GOVERNING LAW & SIGNATURES
25. Retained good-faith resolution, mediation, Texas law, Travis County
    venue, and mandatory non-waivable consumer-rights carve-outs.
26. Added acknowledgments (auto quarterly renewal, 12-hour policy,
    non-cumulative sessions, online-vacation policy, separate waiver,
    electronic-signature validity).
27. Simplified the signature block, at the client's request, to:
    Client Name, Client Signature, Date, Coach Name (Cristy Sim),
    Coach Signature, Date.
